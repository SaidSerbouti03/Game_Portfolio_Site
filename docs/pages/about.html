Said Serbouti

Narrative & Game Designer — DBGA Milan

I'm a first-year Narrative Design student at Digital Bros Game Academy in Milan, working toward a role as a narrative or game designer at studios like CDPR, Techland or Fool's Theory.

Alongside my studies, I work part-time at DHL Express, and I'm building a portfolio that ranges from retro-futuristic cashier sims to branching Twine fiction — aimed at a career path that, over the next few years, points toward Poland.

The thread running through my work is building coherent worlds where mechanics and story hold each other up: a game loop that says something, a line of dialogue that changes depending on how you've already played.
