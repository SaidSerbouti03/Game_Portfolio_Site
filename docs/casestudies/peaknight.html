Peaknight Quest

A peanut knight was created to defeat the evil King Acroma in a dying colourful world.

The concept

A six-day game jam. Pixel art top down dungeon crawler, where the main meccanic is the hit deflection if timed right. My focus was the world and the story: who the protagonist is, where the game takes place, and how the journey begins and ends.

My role
  • Worldbuilding. I built the world of the game. In the world game colour is life force.
  • The protagonist’s story. I wrote it and made it concrete in two screens: an intro screen and an ending screen.
  • Music direction. I helped the composer keep the soundtrack’s tone consistent with the game.
  • Assets. I researched the game assets.
  • UI and screens. I handled the UI, and I created the main menu, win and game over screens.
Working without artists

The team had no artists. For the main menu, win and game over screens I started from references and used AI tools to produce temporary backgrounds. The screens and the UI came together from there.

Telling a story in six days

With so little time, the story had to live in two moments: the intro that sets up who the protagonist is and what they want, and the ending that closes it.

Challenges
  • Telling a complete story, with a beginning and an end, in six days.
  • Delivering menus, UI and a coherent look with no artists on the team.
  • Keeping the soundtrack’s tone in line with the game, coordinating with the team.
