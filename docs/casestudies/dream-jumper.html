Dream Jumper

A liminal horror walking sim where every dream follows its own rules.

The concept

Dream Jumper moves the player through a sequence of first-person dreams, each with its own liminal atmosphere and slightly different rules. It's designed as a demo of 2 to 3 dreams rather than a full game, meant to show range in level design within a short playtime.

My role

I handle general game design and level design: defining each dream's distinct rules, their relative difficulty, and how they fit the project's roguelite structure.

  • Level design for the first dream (current prototype)
  • Defined the distinct rules for each dream
  • Wrote the full game design document

Every dream had to feel familiar and wrong at the same time — the goal was déjà-vu unease, not jump scares.
